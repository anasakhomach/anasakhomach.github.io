## About Me

I’m **Anas Akhomach**, a data scientist and Python developer based in **Tetouan, Morocco**.
I started my career teaching mathematics, then shifted into IT and analytics after realizing how data reveals what words sometimes can’t.

During the COVID-19 lockdown, I began learning programming and analytics. I built dashboards, ran SQL analyses, and automated reporting for local businesses. Over time, I learned to turn complex systems into something simple and useful.

Since then, I’ve worked across projects that mix analytics, automation, and software.
I’ve helped teams find a **40% drop-off in shipping funnels**, reduced daily inventory checks from **4 hours to 15 minutes**, and built **scheduling systems** that plan operations in under five minutes.

My latest project is a **cloud-connected data warehousing system** — built from the ground up.
I designed a **three-layer Medallion Architecture** (Bronze, Silver, Gold), wrote advanced **SQL with CTEs and Window Functions**, used **pandas and SQLAlchemy** for ETL pipelines, and deployed it all to a **serverless PostgreSQL database on Neon**.
It started as a local SQL script and grew into a fully deployed, production-ready application — showing how I connect backend architecture, analytics, and deployment in one workflow.

I like building tools that actually get used — tools that reduce manual work, make data clearer, and leave behind something reliable.
I document what I build so others can use it without me standing over their shoulder.

When I’m not coding, I’m learning languages — I speak **Arabic, English, French, and German (B2)** — and studying how data systems and human systems mirror each other.
